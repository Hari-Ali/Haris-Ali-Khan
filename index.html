A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.

How does a project start?+

Share a short brief: what you do, what your website needs to accomplish, and any timing or budget constraints. We can use that to define the right scope.

Can you redesign an existing website?+

Yes. A redesign starts by understanding what already works, what needs to improve, and how to retain useful content and existing URLs.

What about performance and SEO?+

Clear page structure, metadata, accessible content, responsive design, and efficient assets form the technical foundation. Search rankings also depend on content quality, competition, and ongoing work.

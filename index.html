07 / BEFORE WE BEGIN

A little
more clarity.

Every project is different.
Here’s how we start on the same page.

Can we improve an existing Shopify store?+

Yes. Share your theme, store URL, and the issues you want to solve. We can discuss a focused refinement or a broader redesign without assuming a full rebuild is necessary.

How are pricing and timelines decided?+

They depend on the agreed features, content, integrations, and review needs. Your project brief helps define a tailored scope and quote, with milestones discussed before work starts.

What should I include in my enquiry?+

Your website or store URL, the main goal, design references, and any budget or timing constraints. Brand assets and page content are useful once the scope is clear. Never send account passwords by email.

What about speed and search visibility?+

We prioritize efficient assets, accessible structure, useful metadata, and responsive layouts. Improvements should be measured against your current store. Rankings and sales also depend on content, products, competition, and traffic.

Can we discuss support after launch?+

Include ongoing support in your brief. The scope, availability, and terms can be agreed before launch so you know what is covered.

How many revisions are included?+

Revision rounds are agreed in your project scope. No fixed revision allowance is published until HUR Solution’s package terms are confirmed.

Do you build landing pages?+

Landing pages can be included in a storefront or website brief. Share the campaign, audience, products, and content so we can agree on the right page structure.

How does payment work?+

Payment terms are stated in the proposal and agreement before work begins. No deposit percentage is promised on this site while terms are being finalized.
